After a collision

Symptoms can change in the hours and days after an accident.

Report symptoms accurately to your healthcare professional. Examples of symptoms that may occur after a collision include:

Neck pain & stiffness
Back pain
Headaches
Shoulder pain
Reduced mobility
Muscle soreness
Numbness or tingling
Other new discomfort
